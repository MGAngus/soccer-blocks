Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your iPhone. The game saves automatically after every level. Progress isn't synced between devices, and deleting the app also deletes your progress.

Why are there ads?

Soccer Blocks is free to play. An ad may be shown after you finish a level. There are no ads while you play.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

If you live in a region where consent is required, such as the EU, open the pause menu during a match and tap Privacy settings. To choose whether apps may track you, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your iPhone.

How do I turn off sound or vibration?

Tap the sound button on the title screen, or open the pause menu during a match.

I'm stuck on a level

Every retry deals new pieces, so another attempt plays out differently. Stars depend only on how many moves you use, and any win unlocks the next level.
